A fundamental process for 20–60 day long/short ideas

This site follows the PTM course from top to bottom: macro leading indicators set the portfolio bias, sector screening finds positive and negative outliers, the trade-idea template turns them into ideas, and the portfolio lab keeps risk in check. It updates itself every day from free data.

Roadmap

  1. Phase 0 · Skeleton & automation ✓Live Daily data pipeline, manual data checks, data health page.
  2. Phase 1 · Macro Desk ✓Live Leading indicators (lessons 1–15), four blocks, portfolio bias with overrides, a page per indicator, a Global view by country and region, coincident confirmers, a catalyst calendar and evidence on real-time data.
  3. Phase 2 · Sector Screener ✓Live The 11 numbers, sector averages, outliers, volatility filter, SEC evidence metrics.
  4. Phase 3 · Trade Idea Workbench ✓Live A trade-idea template for every stock (open one from the Screener): price targets with your own assumptions, sector comparison, earnings and catalysts, timing chart, short interest; your idea pipeline.
  5. Phase 4 · Portfolio Lab ✓Live CEW and non-rebalanced backtests, volatility, correlation, beta-adjusted exposure.
  6. Phase 5 · Macro-driven ideas & ADRs ✓Live Macro signals mapped to sectors, ADR screener for international ideas.

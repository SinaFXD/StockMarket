Manual data (entered by you)

ISM, NFIB and the China PMIs have no free API, so they are typed into data/manual/*.csv. A report that has been out for more than 5 days without being entered is shown as awaiting data: it keeps its last date and is left out of the scores. Rows with errors are never used.

Automatic series

Fetched every day from FRED (with ALFRED real-time vintages for revised series) and Yahoo Finance. If a source fails, the last good copy is kept and marked here.

Age = days since the last observation. Trend = last two years (hover or focus for values).
